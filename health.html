🚨 Emergency signs

Contact a veterinarian right away if you see any of these:

  • Difficulty breathing
  • Eating something poisonous
  • Heavy bleeding, collapse or seizures
  • Swollen stomach with no vomiting

Preventive care

  • Yearly checkup at the vet
  • Vaccinations on schedule
  • Flea, tick and worm control
  • Healthy food and regular exercise
