Grooming & Routine

Small daily habits keep your pet happy.

🪮 Brushing

Long coats need daily brushing, short coats weekly.

✂️ Nail trimming

Every 3–4 weeks. Avoid the pink quick.

👂 Ear cleaning

Check weekly. How to spot an ear infection? → Health

🛁 Bathing

Every 4–8 weeks with pet shampoo.

🦷 Dental care

Brush teeth several times a week.

Weekly routine

  • Daily: brushing, fresh water
  • Weekly: ears and teeth
  • Monthly: nails and bath
