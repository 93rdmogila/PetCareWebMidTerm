Grooming & Routine

Small daily habits keep your pet clean, comfortable and happy.

Grooming checklist

🪮

Brushing

Long-haired pets need daily brushing. Short coats need it once a week.

✂️

Nail trimming

Trim every 3–4 weeks. Do not cut the pink part inside the nail.

👂

Ear cleaning

Look inside the ears every week. How to spot an ear infection? → Health

🛁

Bathing

Bathe every 4–8 weeks with shampoo made for pets.

🦷

Dental care

Brush teeth a few times a week to prevent bad breath and gum problems.

Basic equipment

Routine schedule

  • Daily Brushing and fresh water
  • Weekly Check ears and teeth
  • Monthly Trim nails and bathe

Something looks wrong?

Red skin, bad smell or a lot of scratching can be a health problem.
