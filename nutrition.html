Diet & Calories

Feed the right amount for your pet's size and age.

Nutrition overview

A balanced diet keeps your pet's coat shiny, bones strong and weight healthy. How much food a pet needs depends on its size, age and activity level.

  • Choose food made for your pet's species and age
  • Measure portions instead of guessing
  • Always keep fresh water available
  • Change food slowly over about a week

Food categories

  • Dry food (kibble)
  • Wet food (cans, pouches)
  • Fresh or cooked food

Vitamins & fatty acids

  • Omega-3 for coat and joints
  • Vitamins A, D and E
  • Calcium for strong bones

Weight management

  • Weigh your pet every month
  • Limit treats
  • Add daily walks or play

Itchy skin or an upset stomach?

Food can sometimes cause allergies or digestion problems.
